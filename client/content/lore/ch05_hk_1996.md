# Chapter 5 — Hong Kong, 1996

Reference document for chapter-5 content (PLAN §7.6, §12.1). The human-reviewed picture of
1996 Hong Kong; NPC `lore` fields are digests of it. Historical-chapter scope applies
(§7.6): daily life, work, food, family — and only what this file asserts.

## The year as it was (daily-life texture)

- **The emigration wave was real and everyone had a name for it.** Through the early
  nineties, families left — one member first, the rest "later"; shops closed not because
  they failed but because their people had somewhere else to be. Farewell dinners were a
  genre, with photographs and plastic bags of dried seafood for the flight.
- **The countdown was public infrastructure**: clocks and boards counting the days to the
  handover, on buildings and in newspaper corners. Neighbours held different feelings
  about it and mostly talked about something else — rent, school fees, the weather.
- Letters and phone cards carried the distance: an aerogramme took a week, a call home
  cost more than dinner, and a voice on a tape was treasure.
- The street itself thinned and re-tiled; shutter doors stayed down on some fronts while
  others repainted for one more year.

## The fictional layer

- **阿彩 is in her sixties**, still at the counter her family has kept for four decades.
  In 1996 she is writing a letter to her elder sister, who left for Vancouver in 1994 —
  she writes it, seals it, and cannot put a stamp on it, because the address on the
  envelope is one her sister has already left.
- **阿雪 is that sister's daughter**, twenty-something, visiting Hong Kong for the last
  family new year before everyone scatters further. She has never seen the street her
  mother talked about; she is the letter's only possible address.
- **偉哥 is in his thirties here**, newly the building's caretaker, carrying boxes for
  the families that are leaving and keeping count of the ones that stay.
- The letter the player carries from 2027 is this letter — sealed, unstamped, found in
  the flat's drawer twenty years after everyone stopped waiting for it to arrive.

## Rules for this chapter (from §7.6)

- Only assert what this file records. The wave, the countdown, the distances are the true
  record; every person in it is invented.
- The chapter never explains the time travel: 阿彩 recognizes her own handwriting the way
  one recognizes a school exercise book — with a laugh and a shiver, and no questions she
  wants answered.
- The resolution is delivery, not repair: the letter finally gets an address and a
  carrier. What it says stays between the sisters.
