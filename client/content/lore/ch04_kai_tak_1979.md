# Chapter 4 — Kowloon, 1979

Reference document for chapter-4 content (PLAN §7.6, §12.1). The human-reviewed picture of
1979 Kowloon; NPC `lore` fields are digests of it. Historical-chapter scope applies (§7.6):
daily life, work, food, family — and only what this file asserts.

## The year as it was (daily-life texture)

- **Kai Tak's approach was the city's soundtrack.** The checkerboard turn flew airliners
  low over Kowloon rooftops — close enough to read the airline's name — on their final
  descent. Neighbours paused mid-sentence for the roar, then went back to talking.
- **The manufacturing boom was moving families up.** Electronics, watches, garments:
  factory shifts by day, and TV sets, electric fans and refrigerators arriving in homes
  one appliance at a time.
- **Rooftops were the cheap balcony of the city** — laundry lines, coops, a chair facing
  the flight path. Children knew the planes by silhouette before they knew the airlines
  by name.
- A shop that survived its first decade stopped being a gamble and became a fixture: the
  same counter, the same chalk prices, a radio on all day, and the street's trust.
