# Chapter 6 — Hong Kong, 2027 (the finale)

Reference document for chapter-6 content (PLAN §7.6, §12.2). The bookend: the player has
walked 1955 → 1996 and returns to the same street with new eyes. **The design tone for this
chapter is cute** — soft, warm, a little playful; the finale earns its sentiment with light
hands. NPC `lore` fields are digests of this file.

## The street, seen again

- Everything is where it was in chapter 1 — the market, the shop, the flat — but the
  player now knows every stone's story. The chapter never explains the time travel; the
  characters simply feel warmer, as if the street has quietly decided to adopt the player.
- The family's box of mementos is the last decision (§12.2): keep it, bin it, or give it
  back to the street. The cute answer wins: **a little street memory corner** (街坊回憶角)
  built by the shop's door — each keepsake displayed with one line of its story, lanterns
  strung up, milk tea for everyone.
- The tone rules: nobody cries on screen; sentiment arrives as jokes, food, and small
  kind gestures. Exclamations over exclamation marks. The bird gets a singing part.

## The cast (journey-after variants of chapter 1's street)

- **陳太** — softer than ever; she finally says the quiet part out loud: the shop was
  never just a shop, it was the street's kitchen. She crowns the player an honorary
  regular.
- **根叔** — his last word is generous: the street remembers people who remember it, and
  now it remembers the player too. The bird performs.
- **阿明** — the finale's engine of fun: his bright idea is the memory corner with
  lanterns and a street party, planned with the seriousness of a man ordering lunch.
