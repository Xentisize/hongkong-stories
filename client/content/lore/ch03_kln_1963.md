# Chapter 3 — Kowloon, 1963

Reference document for chapter-3 content (PLAN §7.6, §12.1). The human-reviewed picture of
1963 Kowloon; NPC `lore` fields are digests of it. Historical-chapter scope applies (§7.6):
daily life, work, food, family — and only what this file asserts.

## The year as it was (daily-life texture)

- **The water rationing is real history.** In 1963 the reservoirs ran so low that the
  supply came only in windows — a few hours every few days — and the city queued:
  buckets, kettles, baby baths, every container anyone owned, in lines that lasted a
  whole afternoon. Water was boiled, saved, re-used: first for cooking, then for washing.
  Relief came later in the decade when the pipe from the north arrived; in 1963 nobody
  knew that yet.
- **Textile mills were the city's engine.** Spinning and weaving floors ran long shifts;
  the noise was constant and the lint hung in the light. Families took piecework home —
  trimming, packing, sewing — counted and collected by contractors who set the rate.
- A mill wage bought a bunk in a roofed-over rooftop structure or a cubicle in a flat;
  children did homework on shop counters because home was one shared room.
- People measured the year in shipments: an order landed, everyone ate; an order
  slipped, everyone ate plainer.

## The fictional layer

- **阿榮 is the player's father as an eight-year-old.** He waits on the mill floor's
  steps after school, does homework on his mother's cloth bale, and is fiercely proud
  of the coins his own hands earn. He must never be told who the player is.
- His mother — the player's grandmother by 2027's count — pieces work at home for
  **陸經理**, the contractor who sets the trim rate and collects on Fridays. The rate
  dropped this month; the family said nothing, because orders are not argued with.
- **麥嬸** is the lane's queue matriarch: she keeps order at the standpipe, remembers
  whose turn it is, and trades a bucket of water for gossip without ever calling it a
  debt.
- What it cost, this chapter's answer, is double: the family worked for Manager Luk's
  rate, and the whole street paid for water in hours of their lives.
